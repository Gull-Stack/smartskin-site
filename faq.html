📅 Appointments & Preparation

How do I schedule an appointment?
You can schedule an appointment three ways: book online through our website, text us at (385) 273-3376, or call our office directly. We offer same-week appointments for urgent skin concerns.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records. Arriving 15 minutes early to complete paperwork is recommended for new patients.
How do I prepare for my dermatology appointment?
For a general appointment: wear loose, comfortable clothing that allows easy access to your skin. For a full-body skin exam: avoid wearing makeup, nail polish, or hair products that cover your scalp. Bring a list of any spots or moles that concern you.
How often should I see a dermatologist?
We recommend annual skin cancer screenings for most adults. If you have a history of skin cancer, multiple moles, or other risk factors, more frequent visits may be recommended. For chronic conditions like acne, eczema, or psoriasis, follow-up visits are typically scheduled every 3-6 months.

🔍 Skin Cancer & Screenings

What is a skin cancer screening?
A skin cancer screening is a visual examination of your entire body to check for suspicious moles, spots, or growths that could indicate skin cancer. The exam typically takes 10-15 minutes and is painless. We recommend annual screenings, especially if you have fair skin, a history of sunburns, or a family history of skin cancer.
How do I know if a mole is cancerous?
Use the ABCDEs: Asymmetry (one half doesn't match the other), Border (irregular or blurred edges), Color (multiple colors or uneven distribution), Diameter (larger than a pencil eraser), and Evolution (changing in size, shape, or color). If a mole has any of these characteristics, schedule an appointment promptly.
What is Mohs surgery?
Mohs surgery is a precise surgical technique for removing skin cancer that offers the highest cure rate (up to 99%) while sparing the maximum amount of healthy tissue. The surgeon removes thin layers of skin and examines them under a microscope until no cancer cells remain. It's ideal for cancers on the face, ears, hands, and other sensitive areas.
What should I do after a skin biopsy?
Keep the area clean and dry for 24 hours. Apply petroleum jelly and a bandage daily until healed (usually 1-2 weeks). Avoid strenuous activity for 24-48 hours. Some bruising and tenderness is normal. Contact us if you experience excessive bleeding, signs of infection, or have concerns.
Can you remove moles?
Yes, we remove moles for both medical and cosmetic reasons. Suspicious moles are removed and sent for biopsy. Cosmetic mole removal can be done via surgical excision or shave removal depending on the mole's characteristics. Most procedures are quick, performed in-office with local anesthesia.

✨ Cosmetic Treatments

How long does Botox last?
Botox results typically last 3-4 months. Some patients find results last longer with regular treatments over time. We recommend scheduling your next appointment before your results fully wear off to maintain optimal results.
What's the difference between Botox and fillers?
Botox relaxes muscles to reduce wrinkles caused by facial expressions (like forehead lines and crow's feet). Fillers add volume to areas that have lost fullness (like cheeks, lips, and nasolabial folds). Many patients benefit from both treatments used together for comprehensive facial rejuvenation.
What is a chemical peel?
A chemical peel is a treatment that uses a chemical solution to remove damaged outer layers of skin, revealing smoother, more even-toned skin beneath. Peels can improve acne, sun damage, fine lines, and hyperpigmentation. We offer light, medium, and deep peels depending on your skin concerns.
What laser treatments do you offer?
We offer a range of laser treatments including laser hair removal, skin resurfacing, treatment for sun damage and age spots, removal of broken blood vessels, tattoo removal, and treatment for acne scars. Schedule a consultation and our providers will recommend the best laser technology for your specific concerns.

🩺 Medical Conditions

Do you treat acne?
Yes! We offer comprehensive acne treatment for all ages, from teenagers to adults. Treatment options include topical medications, oral medications, chemical peels, laser therapy, and personalized skincare regimens. Our providers will create a customized treatment plan based on your acne type and severity.
What is eczema and how is it treated?
Eczema (atopic dermatitis) is a chronic skin condition causing dry, itchy, inflamed skin. Treatment typically includes moisturizers, topical corticosteroids, and identifying triggers. For moderate to severe cases, we may recommend prescription medications, light therapy, or newer biologic treatments.
What causes rosacea and can it be treated?
Rosacea is a chronic skin condition causing facial redness, visible blood vessels, and sometimes acne-like bumps. While there's no cure, treatments effectively control symptoms. Options include topical medications, oral antibiotics, laser therapy for visible blood vessels, and identifying triggers like sun, alcohol, or spicy foods.
Do you treat children?
Yes, we treat patients of all ages, from infants to seniors. Common pediatric concerns include eczema, birthmarks, warts, molluscum, and acne. Our providers are experienced in making children feel comfortable during their visit.
