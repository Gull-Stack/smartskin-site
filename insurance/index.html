Before Your Appointment

To help us process your insurance claim efficiently, please:

  • Bring your insurance card to every visit
  • Bring a valid photo ID
  • Know your copay amount
  • Inform us of any insurance changes
  • Contact your insurance to verify dermatology benefits

Understanding Your Coverage

Coverage varies by plan and procedure. Here's what to know:

Medical Dermatology

Most insurance plans cover medical dermatology services, including treatment for:

  • Skin cancer screenings and treatment
  • Acne treatment
  • Eczema and psoriasis
  • Rashes and infections
  • Mole evaluations
  • Other medical skin conditions

Cosmetic Services

Cosmetic procedures are typically not covered by insurance, including:

  • Botox and dermal fillers (when cosmetic)
  • Cosmetic chemical peels
  • Cosmetic laser treatments
  • Elective skin rejuvenation

Payment Options

We offer multiple payment options for your convenience:

  • Cash
  • Personal checks
  • Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
  • Health Savings Account (HSA)
  • Flexible Spending Account (FSA)
  • CareCredit financing

Self-Pay Patients

If you don't have insurance or prefer to pay out-of-pocket, we offer competitive self-pay rates. Please contact our office for pricing information.
