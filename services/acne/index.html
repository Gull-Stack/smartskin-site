Professional Acne Treatment

Customized treatment plans for clear, healthy skin. Expert care for teens and adults with all types of acne.

Achieve Clear, Confident Skin

Acne affects nearly 85% of people at some point in their lives. Whether you're a teenager dealing with breakouts or an adult struggling with persistent acne, our board-certified dermatologists offer advanced treatments that go beyond over-the-counter solutions.

Why Professional Acne Treatment?

While drugstore products can help mild acne, many cases require prescription-strength treatments. Professional care offers:

  • Accurate diagnosis of your specific acne type
  • Access to prescription medications not available OTC
  • Treatment of hormonal factors
  • Prevention of permanent scarring
  • Faster, more effective results

Types of Acne We Treat

  • Comedonal Acne: Blackheads and whiteheads from clogged pores
  • Inflammatory Acne: Red, swollen papules and pustules
  • Cystic Acne: Deep, painful nodules and cysts
  • Hormonal Acne: Breakouts related to hormonal fluctuations
  • Adult Acne: Acne persisting into or starting in adulthood
  • Back & Body Acne: Acne on the chest, back, and shoulders

Our Treatment Options

We customize treatment based on your acne type, severity, and individual factors:

Topical Treatments

  • Prescription retinoids (tretinoin, adapalene, tazarotene)
  • Topical antibiotics
  • Benzoyl peroxide preparations
  • Combination therapies

Oral Medications

  • Oral antibiotics for inflammatory acne
  • Hormonal treatments (birth control pills, spironolactone)
  • Isotretinoin (Accutane) for severe, treatment-resistant acne

In-Office Procedures

  • Chemical peels to unclog pores and reduce inflammation
  • Professional extractions
  • Cortisone injections for cystic acne
  • Light and laser therapy

Acne Scar Treatment

If acne has left scars, we offer treatments to improve skin texture:

  • Microneedling to stimulate collagen production
  • Chemical peels for surface irregularities
  • Laser resurfacing for deeper scars
  • Dermal fillers for depressed scars

What to Expect

During your consultation, we will:

  • Evaluate your skin and acne type
  • Discuss your medical history and previous treatments
  • Identify potential contributing factors
  • Create a personalized treatment plan
  • Provide guidance on proper skincare routines
  • Schedule follow-ups to monitor progress

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I see results?

Most treatments take 6-12 weeks to show significant improvement. Consistency is key – stick with your treatment plan and attend follow-up appointments.

Is isotretinoin (Accutane) safe?

Isotretinoin is safe when properly monitored. We closely supervise all patients on this medication with regular blood tests and check-ups. It's highly effective for severe acne.

Will my acne come back after treatment?

Many patients experience long-term improvement, especially with isotretinoin. However, some may need maintenance therapy. We'll work with you to develop a plan for lasting results.

Can you treat acne scars?

Yes! We offer multiple scar treatment options including microneedling, chemical peels, and laser therapy. The best approach depends on your scar type.
