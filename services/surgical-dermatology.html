Surgical Dermatology

Expert surgical care for skin cancer removal, mole excision, and other procedures with minimal scarring.

Precision Surgical Care

Our board-certified dermatologists are highly trained in surgical techniques for removing skin cancers, moles, cysts, and other growths. We use advanced methods to achieve the best possible outcomes while minimizing scarring and preserving healthy tissue.

Surgical Procedures We Offer

  • Skin Cancer Excision: Complete removal of basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and melanoma
  • Mohs Surgery Referrals: For complex skin cancers in cosmetically sensitive areas
  • Mole Removal: Excision of suspicious or cosmetically concerning moles
  • Cyst Removal: Complete removal of epidermoid and pilar cysts
  • Lipoma Removal: Excision of benign fatty tumors
  • Skin Tag Removal: Quick removal of annoying skin tags
  • Wart Removal: Surgical treatment for persistent warts

What to Expect

Most dermatologic surgical procedures are performed in our office under local anesthesia. Here's what you can expect:

  • Pre-procedure consultation to discuss the procedure and answer questions
  • Local anesthesia to ensure your comfort during the procedure
  • Careful surgical technique to minimize scarring
  • Wound care instructions and follow-up appointments
  • Pathology results (for suspicious lesions) within 1-2 weeks

Recovery & Aftercare

Recovery varies depending on the procedure and location. Our team will provide detailed aftercare instructions, which typically include:

  • Keeping the wound clean and dry initially
  • Daily wound care with petroleum jelly and bandages
  • Avoiding strenuous activity for a period
  • Sun protection of the healing area
  • Follow-up for suture removal (if applicable)
