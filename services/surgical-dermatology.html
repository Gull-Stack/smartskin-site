Surgical Dermatology

Expert surgical removal of skin cancers, moles, cysts, and other growths with precision techniques for optimal outcomes.

Precision Surgical Care

At Smart Skin Dermatology, our board-certified dermatologists are highly skilled in surgical procedures to remove skin cancers and other growths. We prioritize complete removal while minimizing scarring and preserving your skin's natural appearance.

Surgical Procedures We Perform

  • Skin Cancer Excision: Complete removal of basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and melanoma
  • Mole Removal: Excision of suspicious or cosmetically concerning moles
  • Cyst Removal: Removal of epidermoid cysts and other benign growths
  • Lipoma Excision: Removal of fatty tissue growths
  • Skin Tag Removal: Quick removal of bothersome skin tags
  • Electrosurgery: Precise removal using electrical current
  • Cryosurgery: Freezing technique for certain growths

Our Surgical Approach

Every surgical procedure at Smart Skin Dermatology follows these principles:

  • Thorough Evaluation: Careful assessment to determine the best surgical approach
  • Local Anesthesia: Comfortable, in-office procedures with local numbing
  • Precise Technique: Complete removal with attention to cosmetic outcomes
  • Pathology Review: Specimens sent for laboratory analysis when indicated
  • Expert Closure: Meticulous wound closure for minimal scarring

What to Expect

Most dermatologic surgeries are performed in our office under local anesthesia. You can expect:

  • Minimal discomfort during and after the procedure
  • Same-day treatment in most cases
  • Clear post-operative instructions for optimal healing
  • Follow-up care to monitor healing and review pathology results

When Surgery Is Recommended

Surgical intervention may be recommended when:

  • A skin cancer is diagnosed and requires removal
  • A mole shows concerning features or changes
  • A growth causes discomfort or irritation
  • Cosmetic improvement is desired
  • Biopsy results indicate the need for complete excision
