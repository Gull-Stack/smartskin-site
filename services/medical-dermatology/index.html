Medical Dermatology

Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for all skin conditions by board-certified dermatologists.

Expert Care for Your Skin Health

At Smart Skin Dermatology, our board-certified dermatologists provide comprehensive medical dermatology services to diagnose and treat conditions affecting your skin, hair, and nails. With over 30 years of combined experience, our team offers personalized treatment plans designed to improve your skin health and quality of life.

Conditions We Treat

Our medical dermatology services address a wide range of skin conditions, including:

  • Acne – Treatment for all types and severities in teens and adults
  • Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis) – Relief from itchy, inflamed skin
  • Psoriasis – Advanced treatments for this chronic condition
  • Rosacea – Management of facial redness and flushing
  • Contact Dermatitis – Identification and treatment of allergic reactions
  • Skin Infections – Treatment for bacterial, viral, and fungal infections
  • Hair Loss (Alopecia) – Diagnosis and treatment options
  • Nail Disorders – Care for fungal infections and other conditions
  • Warts & Molluscum – Removal of viral skin growths
  • Hyperhidrosis – Treatment for excessive sweating

Our Approach to Care

We believe in a patient-centered approach to dermatology. During your visit, we'll:

  • Conduct a thorough examination of your skin concern
  • Discuss your medical history and previous treatments
  • Provide a clear diagnosis and explain all available treatment options
  • Develop a personalized treatment plan that fits your lifestyle
  • Schedule follow-up care as needed to monitor your progress

Advanced Treatment Options

Our clinic offers the latest treatments for skin conditions, including:

  • Prescription topical medications
  • Oral medications including antibiotics and biologics
  • Phototherapy (light therapy)
  • Injectable treatments
  • In-office procedures
