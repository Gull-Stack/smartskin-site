Laser Treatments

Advanced laser technology for hair removal, skin rejuvenation, and treatment of various skin concerns.

Advanced Laser Technology for Beautiful Skin

At Smart Skin Dermatology, we offer a range of laser treatments to address various skin concerns. Our experienced providers use state-of-the-art laser technology to deliver safe, effective results with minimal downtime.

Our Laser Services

  • Laser Hair Removal: Permanent reduction of unwanted hair on face and body
  • IPL Photofacial: Improve sun damage, age spots, and uneven skin tone
  • Laser Skin Resurfacing: Reduce fine lines, wrinkles, and acne scars
  • Vascular Laser: Treat broken blood vessels, spider veins, and rosacea
  • Pigment Laser: Fade dark spots, melasma, and hyperpigmentation
  • Tattoo Removal: Safe removal of unwanted tattoos

Benefits of Medical Laser Treatments

  • Medical Expertise: Treatments supervised by board-certified dermatologists
  • Advanced Technology: State-of-the-art lasers for optimal results
  • Safety First: Proper protocols and experienced providers
  • Customized Care: Treatments tailored to your skin type and concerns

What to Expect

During your laser consultation, we will:

  • Evaluate your skin type and concerns
  • Discuss your goals and expectations
  • Recommend the most appropriate laser treatment
  • Explain the procedure, number of sessions needed, and recovery
  • Provide pre and post-treatment instructions

Preparing for Laser Treatment

General guidelines for laser treatments:

  • Avoid sun exposure and self-tanners for 2-4 weeks before treatment
  • Discontinue certain skincare products as advised
  • Shave the treatment area (for hair removal) before your appointment
  • Arrive with clean skin, free of makeup and lotions
