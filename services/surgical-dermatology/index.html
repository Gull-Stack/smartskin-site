Surgical Dermatology

Expert surgical removal of skin cancers, cysts, moles, and other growths with precision techniques.

Expert Dermatologic Surgery

Our board-certified dermatologists have extensive training and experience in dermatologic surgery. We perform a wide range of surgical procedures to remove skin cancers, benign growths, and other skin lesions with precision and care.

Surgical Procedures We Offer

Skin Cancer Treatment

  • Surgical Excision – Complete removal of skin cancer with clear margins
  • Electrodesiccation & Curettage – For superficial skin cancers
  • Mohs Surgery Referrals – We coordinate care with Mohs surgeons for complex cases

Mole & Lesion Removal

  • Mole Removal – Suspicious or cosmetically concerning moles
  • Skin Tag Removal – Quick, simple removal
  • Lipoma Excision – Removal of benign fatty tumors
  • Benign Tumor Removal – Seborrheic keratoses, dermatofibromas, etc.

Cyst Treatment

  • Cyst Excision – Complete removal of epidermoid and pilar cysts
  • Cyst Drainage – For infected or inflamed cysts

Other Procedures

  • Skin Biopsies – Diagnostic sampling for pathology analysis
  • Ingrown Toenail Surgery – Permanent correction
  • Scar Revision – Improving the appearance of scars

What to Expect

Most dermatologic surgeries are performed in our office under local anesthesia. Here's what you can typically expect:

  • The area is numbed with local anesthetic – you'll feel pressure but no pain
  • The procedure usually takes 15-45 minutes depending on complexity
  • Sutures may be placed and removed 1-2 weeks later
  • You'll receive detailed wound care instructions
  • Most patients return to normal activities the same day

Our Commitment to Excellence

We prioritize both complete removal and optimal cosmetic outcomes. Our surgeons use meticulous technique and careful closure to minimize scarring while ensuring complete treatment of the condition.
