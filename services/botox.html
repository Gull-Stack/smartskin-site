Botox vs. Dysport: Which Is Right for You?

Both Botox and Dysport are highly effective neuromodulators that work by temporarily relaxing targeted facial muscles. While they work similarly, some patients prefer one over the other based on:

  • Treatment area and spread preferences
  • Onset time (Dysport may work slightly faster)
  • Individual response to each product

Our providers will help you determine which product is best suited to achieve your desired results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Botox last?

Botox typically lasts 3-4 months. With regular treatments, some patients find their results last longer over time as the muscles become trained to relax.

Is Botox safe?

Yes, Botox is FDA-approved and has an excellent safety profile when administered by trained medical professionals. It has been used cosmetically for over 20 years with millions of treatments performed worldwide.

What's the difference between Botox and Dysport?

Both are botulinum toxin type A products that work similarly to relax facial muscles. Dysport may spread slightly more and can work faster for some patients. Our providers can help determine which is best for you.

Will I look frozen or unnatural?

Not with our approach! We prioritize natural-looking results that allow you to maintain facial expression while softening lines. We'll work with you to achieve the look you want.
