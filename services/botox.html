Botox & Dysport

FDA-approved wrinkle-relaxing treatments for a refreshed, natural look. Expertly administered by our experienced providers.

Turn Back the Clock, Naturally

Botox and Dysport are FDA-approved injectable treatments that temporarily relax the muscles responsible for expression lines, resulting in smoother, more youthful-looking skin. Our experienced providers deliver natural-looking results that help you look refreshed—never frozen.

Treatment Areas

  • Forehead Lines: Smooth horizontal lines across the forehead
  • Frown Lines (11s): Vertical lines between the eyebrows
  • Crow's Feet: Fine lines around the eyes
  • Bunny Lines: Lines on the nose
  • Lip Lines: Fine lines around the mouth
  • Chin Dimpling: Reduce the orange peel appearance
  • Neck Bands: Soften prominent neck bands

Beyond Wrinkles: Medical Uses

Botox is also FDA-approved to treat:

  • Hyperhidrosis: Excessive sweating in the underarms, palms, or feet
  • TMJ/Jaw Clenching: Reduce jaw tension and teeth grinding
  • Migraines: Help prevent chronic migraine headaches

What to Expect

  • Treatment takes just 10-15 minutes
  • Very fine needles are used—most patients describe minimal discomfort
  • Results appear within 3-7 days and peak at 2 weeks
  • Effects typically last 3-4 months
  • No downtime—return to normal activities immediately

Botox vs. Dysport: Which Is Right for You?

Both products use botulinum toxin type A and work similarly. Some differences include:

  • Dysport may take effect slightly faster (2-3 days)
  • Dysport spreads more, making it ideal for larger areas like the forehead
  • Botox may be more precise for smaller areas
  • Some patients prefer one over the other—we can help you decide

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Botox last?

Botox typically lasts 3-4 months. With regular treatments, some patients find their results last longer over time as the muscles become trained to relax.

Is Botox safe?

Yes, Botox is FDA-approved and has an excellent safety profile when administered by trained medical professionals. It has been used cosmetically for over 20 years with millions of treatments performed.

How much does Botox cost?

Botox is typically priced per unit, ranging from $10-15 per unit. The total cost depends on the treatment area and number of units needed. Most patients use 20-60 units per session. We'll provide an accurate quote during your consultation.

Will I look frozen or unnatural?

When performed by experienced providers like ours, Botox produces natural-looking results. We believe in a conservative approach that softens wrinkles while maintaining your natural expressions.
