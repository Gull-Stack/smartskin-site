Skin Cancer Screening

Early detection is the key to successful skin cancer treatment. Schedule your screening today.

Protect Your Skin Health

Skin cancer is the most common form of cancer in the United States, affecting 1 in 5 Americans by age 70. The good news? When caught early, skin cancer is highly treatable. That's why regular skin cancer screenings are so important.

What is a Skin Cancer Screening?

A skin cancer screening is a visual examination of your skin by a dermatology professional. During the exam, we systematically check your entire body for any suspicious moles, spots, or lesions that could indicate skin cancer or pre-cancer.

What We Look For

  • Basal Cell Carcinoma – The most common type, often appearing as a pearly bump
  • Squamous Cell Carcinoma – Often appears as a scaly red patch or nodule
  • Melanoma – The most serious form, often appearing as an unusual mole
  • Actinic Keratoses – Pre-cancerous rough, scaly patches
  • Atypical Moles – Moles that may have a higher risk of becoming melanoma

The Screening Process

Your skin cancer screening will include:

  • Review of your personal and family skin cancer history
  • Full-body visual examination of your skin
  • Dermoscopy (magnified examination) of any concerning spots
  • Documentation of any lesions requiring monitoring
  • Biopsy of suspicious lesions if needed
  • Discussion of your risk factors and prevention strategies

Who Should Get Screened?

We recommend annual skin cancer screenings for:

  • Adults with a personal history of skin cancer
  • Those with a family history of melanoma
  • People with many moles (more than 50)
  • Fair-skinned individuals who burn easily
  • Anyone with a history of blistering sunburns
  • People who have used tanning beds
  • Those with weakened immune systems
  • Anyone with a new or changing mole

Prevention Tips

  • Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily
  • Wear protective clothing and hats outdoors
  • Seek shade during peak sun hours (10am-4pm)
  • Never use tanning beds
  • Perform monthly self-skin exams at home

🎯 Early Detection Saves Lives

Skin cancer caught early has a 99% survival rate. Schedule your screening today.
