Cosmetic Dermatology

Rejuvenate your skin and enhance your natural beauty with our expert cosmetic treatments in Lehi, Utah.

Look Your Best at Every Age

At Smart Skin Dermatology, we offer a comprehensive range of cosmetic dermatology services designed to help you achieve radiant, youthful-looking skin. Our experienced providers combine artistry with medical expertise to deliver natural-looking results that enhance your unique beauty.

Our Cosmetic Services

  • Chemical Peels: Medical-grade peels to improve skin texture, reduce fine lines, and reveal brighter, more even-toned skin.
  • Microneedling: Stimulate collagen production to reduce acne scars, fine lines, and improve overall skin texture.
  • Dermaplaning: Gentle exfoliation to remove dead skin cells and fine facial hair for smoother, brighter skin.
  • HydraFacial: Deep cleansing, extraction, and hydration for instantly radiant, healthy-looking skin.

Benefits of Professional Cosmetic Care

  • Medical Expertise: Treatments performed by trained medical professionals who understand skin health
  • Customized Approach: Treatment plans tailored to your unique skin type and goals
  • Safe & Effective: Medical-grade products and techniques for optimal results
  • Comprehensive Care: Address both cosmetic and medical skin concerns in one place

What to Expect

During your cosmetic consultation, our providers will:

  • Assess your skin type and current condition
  • Discuss your aesthetic goals and concerns
  • Recommend the most appropriate treatments
  • Explain the procedure, expected results, and recovery
  • Develop a personalized treatment plan

Combining Treatments for Optimal Results

Many patients achieve their best results by combining multiple treatments. For example, pairing Botox with chemical peels can address both dynamic wrinkles and skin texture concerns. Our providers can recommend the ideal combination for your goals.
