Botox & Dysport

Smooth wrinkles and prevent new lines with expert injectable treatments.

Turn Back the Clock with Botox & Dysport

Botox and Dysport are FDA-approved neurotoxin treatments that temporarily relax facial muscles to smooth wrinkles and prevent new lines from forming. At Smart Skin Dermatology, our experienced injectors deliver natural-looking results that refresh your appearance without looking "frozen."

Treatment Areas

  • Forehead Lines – Horizontal lines across the forehead
  • Frown Lines (11s) – Vertical lines between the eyebrows
  • Crow's Feet – Lines around the outer corners of the eyes
  • Brow Lift – Subtle elevation of the eyebrows
  • Bunny Lines – Lines on the sides of the nose
  • Lip Lines – Vertical lines around the mouth
  • Chin Dimpling – "Orange peel" texture on the chin
  • Neck Bands – Platysmal bands on the neck

Botox vs. Dysport

Both products work similarly but have slight differences:

  • Botox – Most well-known brand, precise control, ideal for targeted areas
  • Dysport – Spreads more, great for larger areas like the forehead, may work slightly faster

During your consultation, we'll recommend the best option for your goals.

What to Expect

  • Consultation: We'll discuss your goals and develop a treatment plan
  • Treatment: Takes 10-15 minutes with minimal discomfort
  • Results: Begin appearing in 3-7 days, full effect at 2 weeks
  • Duration: Results last 3-4 months on average
  • No Downtime: Return to normal activities immediately

Medical Uses for Botox

Botox isn't just cosmetic – it's also used to treat medical conditions:

  • Hyperhidrosis – Excessive sweating of underarms, hands, or feet
  • Chronic Migraines – FDA-approved for migraine prevention
  • TMJ/Jaw Clenching – Relieve tension and slim the jawline

Pricing

Botox and Dysport are priced per unit. The number of units needed varies by treatment area and individual. We'll provide a personalized quote during your consultation. Contact us for current pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Botox hurt?

Most patients describe the sensation as a small pinch. We use very fine needles, and treatment is quick – typically just a few minutes for the injections themselves.

Will I look frozen or unnatural?

Not when done correctly! Our goal is natural-looking results that soften lines while preserving your ability to express emotions. We take a conservative approach and can always add more at a follow-up.

How often do I need treatments?

Most patients return every 3-4 months to maintain results. With regular treatments, some patients find they need less product over time.
