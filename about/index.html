Meet Our Team

Our board-certified dermatologists and experienced providers bring over 30 years of combined experience to serve Utah County families.

Dr. David Smart, MD

Board-Certified Dermatologist | Founder

Dr. David Smart founded Smart Skin Dermatology with a vision to provide exceptional, patient-centered dermatological care to Utah County families. With over 25 years of experience, he has built a reputation for clinical excellence and compassionate care.

Dr. Smart specializes in medical dermatology, skin cancer detection and treatment, and surgical dermatology. He is committed to staying at the forefront of dermatological advances to provide his patients with the most effective treatments available.
