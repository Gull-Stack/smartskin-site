Acne Treatment

Clear skin solutions for teens and adults. Our board-certified dermatologists create personalized treatment plans for lasting results.

Achieve Clearer, Healthier Skin

Acne is the most common skin condition in the United States, affecting people of all ages. Whether you're a teenager dealing with breakouts or an adult experiencing hormonal acne, our dermatologists can help you achieve clearer, healthier skin.

Types of Acne We Treat

  • Comedonal Acne: Blackheads and whiteheads caused by clogged pores
  • Inflammatory Acne: Red, swollen pimples and pustules
  • Cystic Acne: Deep, painful cysts that can cause scarring
  • Hormonal Acne: Breakouts related to hormonal fluctuations
  • Adult Acne: Acne persisting or developing in adulthood

Our Acne Treatment Options

We develop personalized treatment plans based on your acne type, severity, and skin type:

  • Topical Medications: Prescription retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, and antibiotics
  • Oral Medications: Antibiotics, hormonal treatments, and isotretinoin (Accutane) for severe cases
  • Chemical Peels: Medical-grade peels to unclog pores and reduce breakouts
  • Extraction: Professional removal of comedones
  • Light & Laser Therapy: Targeted treatments to kill bacteria and reduce inflammation

Acne Scar Treatment

If acne has left scars, we offer treatments to improve skin texture:

  • Microneedling to stimulate collagen
  • Chemical peels for surface irregularities
  • Laser resurfacing for more significant scarring

What to Expect

During your acne consultation, we will:

  • Evaluate your skin and acne type
  • Discuss your skincare routine and lifestyle factors
  • Create a customized treatment plan
  • Provide education on preventing future breakouts
  • Schedule follow-ups to monitor progress and adjust treatment

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes acne?

Acne occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells. Contributing factors include hormones, genetics, bacteria, certain medications, and diet. Our dermatologists can help identify your specific triggers.

Can adults get acne?

Yes, adult acne is very common, especially in women. Hormonal changes, stress, certain medications, and skincare products can all contribute to adult acne. We offer treatments specifically designed for adult skin.

How long does acne treatment take to work?

Most acne treatments take 6-8 weeks to show significant improvement. It's important to be patient and consistent with your treatment plan. We'll monitor your progress and adjust as needed.

Will my acne come back after treatment?

Acne can recur, but with proper maintenance therapy and skincare habits, most patients can keep their skin clear. We'll develop a long-term plan to help prevent future breakouts.
