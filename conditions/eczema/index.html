Expert Care for Eczema & Psoriasis

Eczema and psoriasis are chronic inflammatory skin conditions that can significantly impact your quality of life. At Smart Skin Dermatology, our board-certified dermatologists specialize in diagnosing and treating these conditions with the latest evidence-based approaches.

Understanding Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

Eczema causes patches of skin to become red, inflamed, itchy, and sometimes cracked or rough. It often appears in childhood but can develop at any age. Common triggers include:

  • Environmental allergens (dust, pollen, pet dander)
  • Harsh soaps and detergents
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Weather changes and dry air
  • Certain foods

Understanding Psoriasis

Psoriasis is an autoimmune condition that causes cells to build up rapidly on the skin's surface, forming scales and red patches that can be itchy and sometimes painful. Types we treat include:

  • Plaque Psoriasis: The most common type, causing raised, red patches covered with silvery scales
  • Guttate Psoriasis: Small, dot-like lesions, often triggered by strep infections
  • Inverse Psoriasis: Smooth, red patches in skin folds
  • Pustular Psoriasis: White pustules surrounded by red skin
  • Scalp Psoriasis: Affecting the scalp with thick, crusted plaques

Our Treatment Approach

We offer comprehensive treatment options tailored to your specific condition and lifestyle:

  • Topical Treatments: Prescription-strength corticosteroids, calcineurin inhibitors, and vitamin D analogues
  • Phototherapy: Controlled UV light therapy to reduce inflammation
  • Systemic Medications: Oral or injectable medications for moderate to severe cases
  • Biologic Therapies: Advanced targeted treatments for psoriasis
  • Lifestyle Counseling: Identifying and avoiding triggers

What to Expect at Your Appointment

During your visit, we will:

  • Conduct a thorough examination of affected areas
  • Review your medical history and current symptoms
  • Discuss potential triggers and aggravating factors
  • Create a personalized treatment plan
  • Provide education on proper skincare routines

Frequently Asked Questions

Is eczema contagious?

No, eczema is not contagious. It cannot be spread from person to person. It's related to genetics and immune system function.

Can psoriasis be cured?

While there's no cure for psoriasis, it can be effectively managed with proper treatment. Many patients achieve clear or nearly clear skin with the right therapy.

How long does treatment take to work?

Results vary depending on the treatment and severity. Topical treatments may show improvement in 2-4 weeks, while biologics may take 3-4 months for full effect.
