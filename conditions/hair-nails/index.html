Hair & Nail Disorders

Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for conditions affecting your hair and nails from our expert dermatology team.

Expert Care for Hair & Nail Conditions

Hair and nail disorders can be distressing and may indicate underlying health issues. At Smart Skin Dermatology, our board-certified dermatologists have extensive experience diagnosing and treating a wide range of hair and nail conditions.

Hair Conditions We Treat

  • Androgenetic Alopecia: Male and female pattern hair loss, the most common cause of hair thinning
  • Alopecia Areata: Patchy hair loss caused by autoimmune factors
  • Telogen Effluvium: Temporary hair shedding due to stress, illness, or hormonal changes
  • Traction Alopecia: Hair loss from tight hairstyles or hair treatments
  • Scalp Conditions: Seborrheic dermatitis, scalp psoriasis, and folliculitis
  • Excessive Hair Growth (Hirsutism): Unwanted facial or body hair

Hair Loss Treatment Options

We offer multiple approaches to address hair loss:

  • Topical Medications: Minoxidil and other prescription solutions
  • Oral Medications: Finasteride, spironolactone, and other systemic treatments
  • Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP): Using your body's growth factors to stimulate hair regrowth
  • Corticosteroid Injections: For alopecia areata and inflammatory conditions
  • Lifestyle & Nutritional Counseling: Addressing underlying factors

Nail Conditions We Treat

  • Nail Fungus (Onychomycosis): Fungal infections causing thick, discolored nails
  • Psoriatic Nails: Pitting, discoloration, and nail bed separation from psoriasis
  • Ingrown Toenails: Painful nails growing into surrounding skin
  • Nail Trauma: Injuries affecting nail growth and appearance
  • Nail Dystrophy: Abnormal nail shape or texture
  • Nail Tumors: Benign and malignant growths under or around nails

Nail Treatment Options

  • Topical Antifungals: Prescription-strength nail lacquers and solutions
  • Oral Antifungals: Systemic medications for stubborn infections
  • Laser Therapy: Advanced treatment for nail fungus
  • Surgical Procedures: Nail removal or correction when needed

When to See a Dermatologist

Schedule an appointment if you notice:

  • Sudden or patchy hair loss
  • Excessive shedding or thinning
  • Scalp irritation, redness, or scaling
  • Discolored, thick, or crumbling nails
  • Dark streaks or spots under nails
  • Painful or recurrent ingrown nails

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes hair loss?

Hair loss can result from genetics, hormonal changes, medical conditions, medications, stress, or nutritional deficiencies. A proper diagnosis is essential for effective treatment.

How long does it take to treat nail fungus?

Nail fungus treatment typically takes 3-6 months for fingernails and 6-12 months for toenails, as you must wait for the new, healthy nail to grow out completely.

Is PRP effective for hair loss?

PRP has shown promising results for many patients with androgenetic alopecia, typically requiring 3-4 initial treatments followed by maintenance sessions every 4-6 months.
