Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our dermatology services and what to expect.

Appointments & Scheduling

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office at (385) 273-3376 during business hours. We also offer a contact form on our website for appointment requests.

How soon can I get an appointment?

We typically offer same-week appointments for most concerns. For urgent skin issues, please mention this when calling and we'll do our best to accommodate you as soon as possible.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance card, photo ID, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records. If you have a specific skin concern, it helps to note when it started and any changes you've observed.

Do I need a referral to see a dermatologist?

Most insurance plans don't require a referral for dermatology visits, but some HMO plans do. We recommend checking with your insurance provider to confirm your specific requirements.

Insurance & Payment

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Humana, Medicare, SelectHealth, United Healthcare, and many more. Visit our insurance page for a complete list or call to verify your coverage.

What if I don't have insurance?

We offer competitive self-pay rates for patients without insurance. Please contact our office to discuss pricing and payment options, including payment plans for certain procedures.

Is cosmetic dermatology covered by insurance?

Most cosmetic procedures (Botox, cosmetic peels, etc.) are not covered by insurance. However, some treatments may be covered if they're medically necessary. We'll help you understand what's covered during your consultation.

Services & Treatments

What conditions do you treat?

We treat all conditions affecting the skin, hair, and nails, including acne, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, skin cancer, hair loss, nail disorders, rashes, warts, moles, and more. Visit our conditions page for more information.

Do you offer skin cancer screenings?

Yes! Skin cancer screenings are one of our most important services. We recommend annual full-body skin exams for most adults, especially those with risk factors like fair skin, history of sunburns, or family history of skin cancer.

How long does a typical appointment last?

A standard dermatology visit typically lasts 15-30 minutes. Full-body skin exams may take a bit longer. Cosmetic consultations and procedures vary depending on the treatment.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we welcome patients of all ages. Our dermatologists have experience treating pediatric skin conditions including eczema, birthmarks, warts, and childhood acne.
